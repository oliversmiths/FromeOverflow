The last 90 days

One card per overflow, its state at the last check on the right. The strip below is its last 90 days, one bar a day: red where it discharged, amber for the 48 hours after, green when it was clear, grey before monitoring began.

Where they are

Each monitor placed by its coordinates. Colour is its state at the last check.

    Data from Wessex Water’s near real-time event duration monitor feed, checked every 15 minutes. A monitor records when an overflow starts and stops — not how much was released, and not whether the water is safe. Monitors can trigger on debris or vegetation, so an activation indicates a discharge rather than confirming one. For verified annual totals see the Environment Agency.
