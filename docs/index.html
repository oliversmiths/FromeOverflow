Overflow Watch Frome

    What this is

    A single-town monitor of storm overflows on the River Frome and its tributaries around Frome, Somerset. It checks Wessex Water’s near real-time event duration monitor feed every 15 minutes and keeps its own history – the feed itself publishes only the current status.

    Reading the map

    Each pin is an overflow, coloured by its state at the last check: red discharging now, amber discharged within the last 48 hours, green not discharging, grey no signal. Open the Timeline tab for every monitor’s last 90 days.

    Reading the timeline

    One card per overflow, ranked by total discharge time. The strip is its last 90 days, one bar a day: red where it discharged, amber for the 48 hours after, green when it was clear, grey before monitoring began.

    What it can’t tell you

    A monitor records when an overflow starts and stops – not how much was released, and not whether the water is safe. Monitors can trigger on debris or vegetation, so an activation indicates a discharge rather than confirming one. If a monitor spills twice between two checks only the second is recorded, so the totals are a floor. These figures will not match Wessex Water’s regulator-verified annual returns.
