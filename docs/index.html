    About

    What this is

    A single-town monitor of storm overflows on the River Frome and its tributaries around Frome, Somerset. It checks Wessex Water’s near real-time event duration monitor feed every 15 minutes and keeps its own history — the feed itself publishes only the current status.

    Reading the map

    Each pin is an overflow, coloured by its state at the last check: red discharging now, amber discharged within the last 48 hours, green not discharging, grey no signal. Tap the clock for every monitor’s last 90 days.

    What it can’t tell you

    A monitor records when an overflow starts and stops — not how much was released, and not whether the water is safe. Monitors can trigger on debris or vegetation, so an activation indicates a discharge rather than confirming one. If a monitor spills twice between two checks only the second is recorded, so the totals are a floor.

    These figures will not match Wessex Water’s regulator-verified annual returns.
